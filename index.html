About Arena

A fully air-conditioned world of fun, at Main Karsaz.

Arena is Karachi's family recreational & sports complex — built so you're never bored of repeating the same day-to-day routine. Sixteen bowling lanes, an arcade spread over 5,000 sq. ft., a rodeo bull, climbing wall, bounce arena and courts sit alongside Rangoli, our theme buffet restaurant serving Pakistani, Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Italian, Continental, Lebanese and Arabic food at one table.

Whether it's a Tuesday evening with the kids, a wedding at one of our five banquet halls, a corporate offsite, or a workout at Power House Gymnasium — Arena is built to hold the whole day.
